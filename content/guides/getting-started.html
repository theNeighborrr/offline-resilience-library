01 / Start here

Your library, without a connection.

This starter is a folder of ordinary files. You need a powered computer, a way to connect the drive, and a web browser to read these included pages.

Open it

  1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder. Keep its subfolders together.
  2. Double-click START-HERE.html. It opens in your usual browser.
  3. Choose an item marked Included offline. Use the Library link to come back.
  4. Press Ctrl+P to print any worksheet. These are blank paper forms; they do not save typed information.

Know what you have

Version 0.1 contains this guide plus household contact, inventory, and communications worksheets. Items marked To add are future sources. Clicking their source links needs internet and does not install the content.

Homepage search looks through catalog descriptions and keywords. It does not search inside books or archives. Future ZIM archives need a compatible reader such as Kiwix; EPUB books and specialized map files may also need their own readers.

Copy and test

  • Copy the whole extracted folder to your USB drive.
  • Open START-HERE.html from the USB copy.
  • Disconnect Wi-Fi and unplug wired networking; open all four included pages.
  • Print one worksheet and check that its table fits the paper.
  • Safely eject, reconnect, and open the drive again.
  • Try a second computer before relying on the collection.

A USB drive stores information; it does not supply a screen, a computer, or power. Keep a printed copy of key plans where your household can find it.

If something does not open

  • Blank or unstyled page: extract the ZIP fully and make sure the assets folder is beside START-HERE.html.
  • Missing file: copy the entire folder again. Avoid moving individual files.
  • Search unavailable: enable JavaScript for this local page, or browse the complete list directly.
  • A source website fails: source links are online research links, not included files.

Keep the shared copy clean

Share blank worksheets. Keep completed contact sheets, account information, home details, and other personal records in a separate private location. The starter does not encrypt files. Recheck contents and printouts when household information changes.
